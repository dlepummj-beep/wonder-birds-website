What we do

Wonder Birds aggregates live birds from contracted producers, has them processed under contract, and sells branded fresh and frozen chicken across twelve product lines. Our edge is the brand, the buyer relationships and the cold-chain distribution platform, backed by a supply chain we control through written agreements.
